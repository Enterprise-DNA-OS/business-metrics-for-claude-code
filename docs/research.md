## Annual bill

The [Tableau pricing selector](https://www.tableau.com/product-and-pricing-selector) lists Enterprise Creator at AUD161, Explorer at AUD98 and Viewer at AUD49 per user per month, billed annually. At least one Creator is required. Taxes are excluded and the calculator warns that its result may not be the final price.

An illustrative 50-person mix, one Creator, ten Explorers and 39 Viewers, costs AUD36,624 per year: (161 + 10 x 98 + 39 x 49) x 12. This ranks the candidate; it is not a verified invoice, typical spend or package quote. Public copy uses individual vendor rates and their twelve-month equivalents only. Available local customer records contained no named Tableau buyer. Live CRM was not accessed, so buyer evidence scores 2. Company identity and inventory showed no Tableau resale offer.
